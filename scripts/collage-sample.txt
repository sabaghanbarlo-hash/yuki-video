# Why you cannot sleep

Hey, have you ever lain in bed at night, completely exhausted, and still your brain would not switch off? [pose: wave]
It feels like someone flipped a light switch inside your head, and now it is stuck on.
Most people blame stress, or coffee, or the phone. But there is another reason that nobody talks about.
Your brain is a prediction machine, and at night it starts running fake scenarios. [text: Fake scenarios]
It replays an awkward conversation from three years ago, and then it rehearses a meeting that has not even happened yet.
Scientists call this "rumination", and it can keep you awake for hours.
That makes you feel anxious, which keeps your mind busier, which makes the whole cycle worse!
Here is the surprising part. Studies found that about 70 percent of people with insomnia say their mind races before bed.
So what actually works? Maybe it is not trying harder to sleep.
The trick is to give your brain a different job. Write down tomorrow's worries, stretch, and let your body slow down.
That is it. Your brain is not broken, it is just working too hard for you.
