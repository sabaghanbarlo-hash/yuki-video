Imagine waking up tomorrow and looking up at the sky. The moon is gone. Completely vanished.

At first, you might think nothing has changed. The sun still rises, the stars are still there, and life goes on.

But things would start getting strange pretty quickly.

Without the moon's gravity, ocean tides would become much weaker. Coastal ecosystems would begin to change, and countless marine animals that depend on the tides would struggle to survive.

Nights would also become much darker. No more moonlight illuminating forests, oceans, or empty streets.

But here's the really scary part.

The moon helps stabilize Earth's rotation. Without it, over thousands or millions of years, Earth's tilt could become increasingly unpredictable, causing extreme climate changes and potentially devastating seasons.

And there's one more thing we'd lose: solar eclipses and lunar eclipses would become impossible.

So, would humanity go extinct tomorrow if the moon disappeared?

Probably not.

But our planet would slowly become a very different, darker, and much less stable place.
