Have you ever replayed a conversation in your head, wondering if you said something wrong?

That's called overthinking.

Psychologically, overthinking is often your brain's attempt to gain control over uncertain situations.

You keep analyzing the past or imagining the future, hoping to find the perfect answer.

But here's the problem: thinking more doesn't always mean understanding more.

Sometimes, the best thing you can do for your mind is accept that not everything needs an explanation.

Remember, you don't have to solve every thought that crosses your mind.
