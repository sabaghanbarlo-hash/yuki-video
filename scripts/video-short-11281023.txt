Sometimes you don't fall for the person. You fall for the possibility of them. Your brain starts building an entire future from three conversations, one compliment, and a suspiciously long eye contact session. You imagine who they could be, what your life might look like together, and how perfectly everything would work. But you're often getting attached to a fantasy your brain created before you actually knew them. That's why sometimes losing someone you barely knew can hurt so much. You're not just losing them. You're losing the future you imagined.
