# The Deepest Place on Earth

## The descent
[style: voxel]
Imagine sinking in a tiny submarine. At first, the water is bright blue and full of fish.
At 200 meters, the sunlight fades. At 1000 meters, it is completely dark and the cold is crushing.

## The bottom
[style: toon]
At the very bottom of the Mariana Trench, 11 kilometers down, the pressure is more than 1000 times what you feel at the surface. A single crab-sized creature there lives under the weight of a thousand elephants.

## Life in the dark
Nobody expected to find life here. But the scientists were wrong, and the deep sea is full of strange, glowing animals.
