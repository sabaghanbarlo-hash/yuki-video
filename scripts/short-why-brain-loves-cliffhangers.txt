Why does your brain refuse to let a cliffhanger go?
Psychologists call it the Zeigarnik effect. In the 1920s, Bluma Zeigarnik noticed that waiters remembered unpaid orders perfectly, then forgot them the moment the bill was settled.
An unfinished task stays open in your mind. It keeps nagging you until it is closed.
That is why a show ends on a cliff, and you press play on the next episode.
It also works for you. Stuck on a big task? Start it for just five minutes. Your brain will not want to leave it half done.
Open loops pull you in. Use them on purpose.
