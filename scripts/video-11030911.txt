Have you ever wondered why sleep is so important for your brain?

Sleep isn't just about resting. It's when your brain gets to work.

While you sleep, your brain processes emotions, organizes memories, and removes waste that builds up during the day.

That's why a lack of sleep can make you more emotional, less focused, and even more stressed.

Interestingly, sleep also plays a major role in learning. When you study something new, your brain strengthens those memories while you sleep.

So, the next time you feel guilty about going to bed early, remember: you're not wasting time. You're giving your brain the chance to recharge, heal, and become stronger.
