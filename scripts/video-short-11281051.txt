Ever notice how someone becomes ten times more attractive the second they stop giving you attention? Your brain loves uncertainty. When someone's attention is unpredictable, you can become more focused on getting the next bit of validation. A message here, a compliment there, a sudden disappearance followed by a return. It creates a cycle of anticipation that can make you confuse anxiety with attraction. Sometimes you're not obsessed with the person. You're obsessed with trying to figure out where you stand.
