Your brain has apparently decided that 2 AM is the perfect time to investigate something you said three years ago. You replay the conversation, imagine better responses, and somehow convince yourself that one awkward sentence permanently ruined your reputation. But most people aren't remembering your mistakes. They're busy replaying their own. Your brain isn't necessarily trying to torture you. It's trying to learn from social experiences and prevent future embarrassment. Unfortunately, it has the efficiency of a detective investigating a case that's already been closed.
