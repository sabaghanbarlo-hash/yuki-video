Why does a year feel shorter every time you get older?
One idea is simple maths. When you are ten, one year is ten percent of your whole life. At fifty, it is two percent. A smaller slice feels faster.
Another idea is memory. A new place, a new school, a first anything fills your brain with fresh memories, so that time feels long and full.
Adult life repeats itself. Same commute, same desk, same screen. Your brain stores less, and the months blur together.
The fix is not a secret. Do new things. A new route, a new hobby, a trip. Make more memories, and you stretch time back out.
