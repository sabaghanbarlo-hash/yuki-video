Are you the same person you were ten years ago?
Here is an old puzzle from ancient Greece. The ship of Theseus sails for years. Every time a plank rots, the crew replaces it.
Eventually, every single plank has been swapped. Is it still the same ship?
Now look at you. Almost all the cells in your body are replaced over the years. Your beliefs changed. Your habits changed. Even your memories shift every time you recall them.
So what stays? Some say it is your story. Some say it is your pattern, not your parts.
Maybe you are not a thing at all. Maybe you are a ship that keeps sailing.
