Some people would rather struggle for six hours than ask someone for five minutes of help. Not because they're incapable of accepting help, but because somewhere along the way, they learned that depending on others was uncomfortable, unsafe, or disappointing. So they become extremely independent. They solve everything themselves. They never complain. But eventually, independence can become a prison when you convince yourself that needing someone is the same as being weak. It isn't. You're human. You weren't designed to carry everything alone.
