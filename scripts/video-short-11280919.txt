Have you ever noticed that you're a completely different person depending on who you're around? Around one friend, you're loud and chaotic. Around another, you're quiet and serious. Around your family, you're somehow 12 years old again. This doesn't necessarily mean you're fake. Humans naturally adjust their behavior to different social environments. The real question isn't whether you change around people. It's whether you're changing because you're comfortable, or because you're afraid they'll reject the real you.
