# Why you cannot sleep

Ever lie in bed exhausted, and your brain just will not switch off? [pose: wave]
Your brain is a prediction machine, and at night it runs fake scenarios. [text: Fake scenarios]
It replays an awkward chat from three years ago, then rehearses a meeting that has not happened yet.
Scientists call this "rumination".
About 70 percent of people with insomnia say their mind races before bed.
The fix? Give your brain a different job. Write down tomorrow's worries, stretch, and let your body slow down.
Your brain is not broken. It is just working too hard for you.
