# 5 Mind-Blowing Facts About Space

## Fact one
A day on Venus is longer than its year. Venus spins so slowly that one rotation takes 243 Earth days, but it circles the Sun in only 225.

## Fact two
There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on every beach on Earth. Astronomers estimate about 200 billion trillion stars.

## Fact three
If you could drive a car straight up, you would reach space in about one hour. The edge of space is only 100 kilometers away.

## Fact four
Neutron stars are so dense that a teaspoon of one would weigh about 6 billion tons.
