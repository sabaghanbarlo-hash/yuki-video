# Why You Keep Starting Over

## The Reset Loop

You know that feeling when you promise yourself that Monday is the day everything changes? New planner, new habits, new you. And then by Thursday, it all quietly falls apart.

[loop: The reset loop :: Big plan | Burnout | Guilt | Restart] Here is the loop. You make a big plan, you burn out, you feel guilty, and then you restart again.

Here is the thing nobody tells you. The problem was never your discipline. The problem is that you built a plan for the person you wish you were, not the person who actually shows up tired on a Tuesday night.

## The Small Fix

[compare: What you plan :: Gym 7 days | Wake at 5 AM | No sugar || What works :: Walk 5 minutes | Sleep earlier | Add one veggie] So instead of a perfect routine, try a boring one. One tiny action you can do even on your worst day.

Two minutes is all it takes. The goal is not progress, it is proof that you still keep promises to yourself.

[list: The 2-minute rule :: Pick one tiny action | Do it for two minutes | Stop and repeat tomorrow] Here is the whole rule. Pick one tiny action. Do it for two minutes. Then stop, and repeat it tomorrow.
