# Why You Keep Starting Over

## The Reset Loop

You know that feeling when you promise yourself that Monday is the day everything changes? New planner, new habits, new you. And then by Thursday, it all quietly falls apart.

Here is the thing nobody tells you. The problem was never your discipline. The problem is that you built a plan for the person you wish you were, not the person who actually shows up tired on a Tuesday night.

## The Small Fix

So instead of a perfect routine, try a boring one. Two minutes. One tiny action you can do even on your worst day. The goal is not progress, it is proof that you still keep promises to yourself.
