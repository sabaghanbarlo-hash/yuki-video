# The Day I Locked Myself Out

[cast: Mia = girl | Tom = boy | Mrs. Lee = woman]

## Monday morning
So this is the story of the worst Monday of my life. I woke up late, grabbed my keys, and ran out the door.
[sfx: door_slam] Mia: Okay, bye apartment! I am so late for work!
Then I heard it. The lock clicked behind me, and my keys were still inside.

Mia (shocked): No. No no no. You are kidding me!
Her neighbour, Tom, came out and laughed so hard he almost dropped his coffee.
Tom (happy): Haha, did you lock yourself out again?
Mia (angry): This is not funny, Tom!

## The rescue
Suddenly the phone rang. It was the landlady, Mrs. Lee, and she had a spare key.
Mrs. Lee: Do not worry, dear. I am on my way.
Mia (sad): Thank you so much. I thought my whole day was ruined.
Mrs. Lee (happy): Nonsense. Every great story starts with a locked door.
In the end she got to work only an hour late, and honestly, she finally learned to check her pockets. That is it. See you in the next story!
