# Shorts ideas for Yuki's Mind Lab

Each is a 30-60 second script (80-150 words): hook in the first sentence, one idea, a closing twist. Paste one into the page, pick **Short**.
The thumbnail follows the hook, so phrase the first line as a question or a shock.

## Psychology
1. Why you cannot sleep (sample: scripts/short-sample.txt)
2. Why your brain loves cliffhangers (the Zeigarnik effect)
3. The spotlight effect: nobody is watching you as much as you think
4. Why you remember embarrassing moments for years
5. Decision fatigue: why you eat badly at night
6. The Dunning-Kruger effect in 40 seconds
7. Why time speeds up as you get older
8. Why you hear your name in a noisy room (the cocktail party effect)

## Philosophy

11. Why Sisyphus was happy (Camus)
12. The trolley problem, 30-second version
13. Memento mori: why remembering death helps you live

## Science and body
14. Why yawning is contagious
15. What happens to your brain when you scroll for an hour
16. Why you get déjà vu
17. Why we cannot tickle ourselves
18. The 70 percent rule: how much of your day runs on autopilot

## Series formats (repeatable, good for the algorithm)
- "Why do we ...?" (one everyday mystery, one answer)
- "3 things your brain does while you sleep"
- "Myth or fact?" (a belief, then the twist)
- "One idea a day" (a single concept, always the same closing line)
